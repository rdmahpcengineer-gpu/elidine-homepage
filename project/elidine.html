01The Atelier

An artisan's approach to the African crown.

Elidine was founded by Elidine — a true American-dream story. A minority woman-owned business, bootstrapped from a dorm room into a Texas atelier, one chair at a time.

Elidine arrived in the U.S. as a design student — originally chasing a career in interior design. She braided her classmates' hair after class to pay tuition. Word traveled. The dorm became a salon. The salon became a waiting list. The waiting list became the kind of little black book celebrities and stylists quietly pass around when they want a crown that photographs like couture.

Today, every appointment is treated like a fitting at a fashion house. One client at a time. Clean parts. Scalp-first technique. Hair pre-stretched, products laid out, a candle lit, and tea on the side. We don't rush the crown.

Whether you're walking into a wedding, a board meeting, a stadium, or your own front door — you'll leave with a style that respects your hair and looks like you just stepped off the cover.
