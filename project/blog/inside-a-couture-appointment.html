The Deposit, the Consult, the Chair: What a Couture Appointment Feels Like

A private, by-appointment studio is a different experience by design.

January 5, 20266 min read

Booking a private, by-appointment atelier is a different experience than dropping into a salon, and it is different on purpose. Every part of the process — from the deposit to the consultation to the pace of the chair itself — is designed to make the work unhurried and the result personal. Here is what actually happens when you book with us.

01The deposit: what it really is

A deposit can feel like a hurdle, so it is worth explaining plainly. Our deposit is non-refundable, but it is transferred in full to your service when you arrive — it is not an extra fee, it is simply the first part of your payment, held in advance. What it buys is certainty: it reserves a dedicated block of time in a single-client studio, so your seat is genuinely yours and the day is planned around you.

02The consultation: before a single braid

Every appointment begins with a real conversation. We talk through the style you want, look honestly at your hair type and length, discuss how long you plan to wear it, and settle on size, parting, and finish together. This is where a good result is actually decided. Ten minutes of honest consultation prevents the small regrets that surface a week later.

The chair is single-client for a reason. Your time is not being split with anyone else’s.

On the atelier model

03The chair: unhurried by design

Braiding done well takes hours, and we do not pretend otherwise. In the chair, the pace is deliberate — this is not an assembly line, and you are not being rushed toward the next client. Come having eaten, bring water, and settle in. The unhurried pace is precisely what allows the precision the finished style depends on.

04Leaving with a plan

You do not just leave with braids; you leave with a plan for keeping them beautiful. Before you go, we walk through the aftercare ritual for your specific style and set expectations for how long it should last. The appointment is the beginning of the relationship with your hair, not the end of it.

A couture appointment asks a little more of you up front — a deposit, a consultation, a few unhurried hours. In return it gives you something a rushed chair never can: a style built entirely around you.
