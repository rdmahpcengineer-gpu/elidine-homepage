Faux Locs: Butterfly, Soft, or Distressed — A Field Guide

The look of locs, on your own timeline.

March 29, 20267 min read

Faux locs are the answer to a very common wish: to wear the beauty of locs without beginning a years-long loc journey. They are installed, worn for weeks, and taken down — no permanence, all of the aesthetic. But faux locs are not one style. They are a family, and the finish you choose changes everything.

01Soft locs

Soft locs are the most polished of the three — wrapped smooth and supple so they bend and move rather than standing stiff. They read refined and comfortable, and their pliancy makes them easy to style into buns and updos. If you want faux locs that look elegant and feel gentle, this is the starting point.

02Distressed locs

Distressed locs lean into texture on purpose — deliberately roughed up along their length for a lived-in, organic look that mimics the character of a mature natural loc. They are the most casual and the most forgiving, since a little fray only adds to the effect. This is the bohemian, unbothered end of the spectrum.

03Butterfly locs

Butterfly locs get their name from the soft, looped texture worked into the loc, giving them a distinctive coiled, airy appearance with little wisps throughout. They sit somewhere between soft and distressed — textured, but intentional about it. They have become a signature look for good reason: they are unmistakable.

Soft locs are tailored, distressed locs are lived-in, and butterfly locs are somewhere poetic in between.

On the three finishes

04Choosing well

A few honest questions make the decision easy:

  • Do you want polished or undone? Soft leans polished, distressed leans undone.
  • How much daily fuss can you tolerate? Distressed is the most carefree.
  • How long will you keep them? Longer wears favor a finish that ages gracefully.

05Comfort and care

Whichever finish you choose, faux locs carry length and therefore weight, so we install them with attention to the root and the edges. At night, a satin scarf or bonnet keeps the texture intact and the hairline protected. Treated with a light hand, a good set of faux locs looks freshly installed well past the point most people expect.
