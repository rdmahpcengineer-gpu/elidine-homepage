How to Prep Your Hair Before a Braiding Appointment

A little preparation buys you a faster, gentler, better install.

February 1, 20265 min read

How your hair arrives at the atelier quietly determines how the whole appointment goes. Well-prepared hair braids faster, sits more comfortably, and lasts longer. Under-prepared hair costs everyone time and can compromise the finished style. The good news: preparing well takes very little, and it makes a real difference.

01Wash — and truly dry

Arrive with freshly washed hair, cleansed of product buildup and, crucially, fully dry. Damp hair braids poorly and can lead to that dreaded mildew smell down the line. A clean, dry canvas is the foundation of a clean, durable install. If you are unsure how far in advance to wash, the day before is usually ideal.

02Detangle thoroughly

This is the step that saves the most time in the chair. Hair should be completely detangled, from ends to roots, before you sit down. Knots and tangles slow the braiding dramatically and create uneven tension. Take your time with a good conditioner and a wide-tooth comb the night before — your appointment (and your scalp) will move far more smoothly for it.

Ten quiet minutes of detangling at home saves an hour of pulling in the chair.

On preparation

03Come with a reference — and an open mind

If you have a specific look in mind, bring a photo. It gives us a shared starting point for the consultation. Just hold it loosely: your hair type, length, and face shape may call for adjustments that make the style work better on you than on the person in the picture. The best results usually live a small distance from the reference.

04Plan for the time

Braiding is unhurried work by nature, and good braiding especially so. Come having eaten, bring water, and settle in — a rushed client makes for a rushed style, and rushed styles are the ones we all regret. If you have somewhere to be, tell us up front so we can plan the session honestly rather than racing the clock at the end.

Show up clean, dry, detangled, and unhurried, and you have already done your part to guarantee a beautiful result. The rest is ours.
