Passion vs. Senegalese Twists: Choosing Your Texture

Two twists, two entirely different moods.

April 12, 20266 min read

Twists are often lumped together, but the two most requested — passion twists and Senegalese twists — could hardly be more different in spirit. Choosing between them is less about which is prettier and more about which mood you want to wear for the next several weeks.

01Passion twists: soft and romantic

Passion twists are made with a wavy, textured hair that gives them a lived-in, bohemian softness. They look tousled in the best way — full of movement, a little undone, endlessly touchable. They read younger and more relaxed, and they forgive a bit of frizz as part of their charm rather than a flaw.

02Senegalese twists: sleek and defined

Senegalese twists are the polished sibling. Made with smoother hair twisted into rope-like, defined columns, they read sleek, elegant, and deliberately finished. Where passion twists whisper, Senegalese twists state. They are the choice when you want something that looks crisp and composed from across a room.

Passion twists look like a Sunday morning. Senegalese twists look like an evening out.

On choosing a texture

03How they wear over time

Their aging tells you a lot about the commitment:

  • Passion twists embrace frizz, so they age gracefully and stay easy for weeks.
  • Senegalese twists show wear more plainly, because their whole appeal is in the smooth definition.
  • Both benefit enormously from a satin bonnet — but Senegalese twists genuinely depend on it.

04Which one is you?

If you want a style that looks effortless, moves softly, and asks little of you day to day, passion twists are the answer. If you want a defined, elegant finish and you are willing to protect it a little more carefully, Senegalese twists reward that care. In consultation we will look at your hair type, your routine, and how much upkeep genuinely fits your week — because the right twist is the one you will still love in week four.
